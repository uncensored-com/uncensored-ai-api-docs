## When to use Discord

- API integration questions
- Bug reports and unexpected behavior
- Feature requests
- Rate-limit tier upgrades for hobby projects
- Showing off what you've built

## Enterprise & higher limits

For Enterprise tier rate limits, custom pricing, or commercial agreements,
post in the `#enterprise` channel on [Discord](https://discord.gg/buutYkX7XT)
and a team member will DM you.

## Status & incidents

Operational status and incident updates are also posted in the
`#status` channel on [Discord](https://discord.gg/buutYkX7XT).
