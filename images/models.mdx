Default model: `model-dev`. The API can default `model` when omitted, but `model-dev` (Flux Dev) is the oldest model in this catalog, so always send `model` explicitly and pick a current model such as `nano-banana-2`, `flux-2-pro` or `seedream-v5`.

`common` means `image_width`, `image_height`, `callback_url`, `resolution`, `quality`, `additional_settings`, and `enable_fallback`. Model-specific knobs such as `seed`, `negative_prompt`, `aspect_ratio`, `num_inference_steps`, `guidance_scale`, and `output_format` are passed in `additional_settings` unless they are documented as top-level convenience params.

Models are listed newest first. A `Not published` cost means the catalog returns no price for that model yet; call [`POST /v1/billing/estimate`](/platform/billing#estimate-cost) for the amount that will be held on your balance.
