<Badge color="green" shape="pill">Live</Badge>

The full matrix of video models. Use the `Model ID` exactly as shown when sending [video generation](/video/generation) requests.

`common` means `callback_url`, `additional_settings`, and `enable_fallback`. Model-specific settings such as `seed`, `negative_prompt`, and `generate_audio` are passed in `additional_settings`. `generate_audio` is only supported on audio-capable models (those that list it below); setting `generate_audio: true` adds an audio track and roughly doubles the per-second cost. The cost shown below is the default audio-off rate, as returned by [`POST /v1/billing/estimate`](/platform/billing#estimate-cost); per-second prices are multiplied by `seconds`, and where no resolution is listed the price applies to all. For models with a fallback chain it is the highest price in the chain, so the actual charge can be lower.

Models are listed newest first. A row marked <Badge color="orange" shape="pill">Unlisted</Badge> is still documented but is no longer returned by `GET /v1/models?type=video`, so do not pick it for a new integration. A `Not published` cost means the catalog returns no price for that model yet; call [`POST /v1/billing/estimate`](/platform/billing#estimate-cost) for the amount that will be held on your balance.
